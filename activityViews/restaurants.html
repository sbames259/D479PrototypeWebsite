Restaurants

Taniti currently has 10 restaurants: five serve mostly local fish and rice, three serve American-style meals, and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

  • Restaurant 1: A dock-side cafe that serves fresh seafood.
  • Restaurant 2: A small serve-and-go in the open-air seafood market.
  • Restaurant 3: A casual dining seafood restaurant that caters to couples.
  • Restaurant 4: An out of the way seafood cafe near the volcano.
  • Restaurant 5: An upscale seafood dining experience in the middle of the city.
  • Restaurant 6: An American burger joint in downtown Taniti City.
  • Restaurant 7: An expensive Steakhouse.
  • Restaurant 8: A casual dining restaurant that serves breakfast all day.
  • Restaurant 9: A high-end sushi restaurant.
  • Restaurant 10: A small shack that serves noodles.
